BOQ & Cash Flow

Budgets and payments for the Timetable's items. New items are added on the Timetable page.

Bill of Quantities

Daily Workers

From each daily log: daily workers × that day's rate. Paid by the month - Pay records what was paid.

Guards

From each daily log: guards × that day's guard rate. Paid by the month - Pay records what was paid.

Equipment Rentals

Daily price × days. The cost counts day by day from the start date; Pay records what was paid to the supplier.

Materials & Purchases

Everything the client buys for the site. A material can name its job, so it counts against that job's materials budget and shows who it was supplied to. Tools and equipment the site keeps (a laser level, a measuring tape) and other purchases are for the whole site.

Monthly Cash Flow

Planned = each item's budget (contract + materials) spread evenly over its planned dates. Spent = contract payments, daily workers, guards, equipment rentals and materials bought that month. The cumulative columns are running totals - compare them to see if spending is ahead of or behind plan.

Finance

What the project earns against what it costs. A loan or the client's own money is funding, not income.

Forecast of the final cost

What the project will have cost when it is finished, from what is known today. An item counts at its budget, or at what has really gone into it when that is more; daily workers, guards and rentals carry on at the rate kept so far until the planned completion.

Money in

Every sum that came in. Income - the employer's payments, buyers' installments - counts toward profit. Funding - a loan, the client's own money, a partner's - pays for the work but is never profit; a loan's interest is a cost.

Work done

No quantity: the work went on that day, unmeasured - it shows as in progress. A quantity: you measured it that day, and measurements add up in the project report. The list holds every work used in this project, so the same work adds up in the project report (200 m² + 300 m² = 500 m²). A work leaves the list once no entry uses it. For a new one, choose + New work and write it in one language - the other is filled in for you; check it before saving.
